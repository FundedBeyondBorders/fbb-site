FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FundedBeyondBorders a broker?

No. We are an educational trading-evaluation platform. All trading happens in a simulated environment — no order is ever sent to a live financial market.

What happens if I fail a phase?

The challenge account status becomes 'breached' and you can no longer trade on it. You can purchase a new challenge and try again.

What leverage do you offer?

Leverage varies by market category (crypto is lower, forex is higher — you'll see the exact number next to each symbol on the platform itself). If your floating loss exceeds your free margin, your positions are automatically closed (a margin call) — full details are on the Rules page.

How is funded-account profit paid out?

When you request it, your profit share (per the rules) is instantly credited to your internal wallet in your dashboard. From there, you can submit a withdrawal request for real USDT (TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20) to your own address, which we send manually.

Why do I need to submit KYC?

Only before your first payout request — to make sure the payout actually reaches the person who opened the account. You can trade freely before that.

Why do you only accept USDT?

USDT has no price volatility, transaction fees (especially on TRC20 and BEP20) are very low, and it's accessible almost everywhere — this keeps deposits, withdrawals, and internal accounting simple and predictable.

What's the difference between the free test account and a real challenge?

The test account is only for feeling the spread and commission — it has no profit target or loss limit and can never become funded. To actually take part in the evaluation and reach funded capital, you need to buy one of the challenge plans. You can try it right now, with no signup required, from the 'Free Test Account' button at the top of this page.

How long does order verification take?

Verifying the exact payment amount is always done manually by our team (for extra security), usually within a few hours. If it's taking longer, follow up via a support ticket.

Can I have more than one account?

Yes, up to 3 active accounts at once, with a combined total of up to $750,000. Full details are on the Rules page.

What is Instant Funding?

A higher-priced plan that funds your account immediately, with no evaluation phase at all — you can start trading right away with no phase to pass. (Note: the standard 14-day minimum before your first payout still applies here, same as every other plan.) In exchange, your profit share (50%) is lower than the regular plans and the loss limits are more conservative. It suits traders who want to start right away and are willing to pay more for it.

What is two-factor authentication (2FA) and how do I enable it?

An extra security layer — even if your password leaks, no one can log into your account without a code from an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator). From your dashboard's Account Security section, tap enable and scan the code into your app. We strongly recommend turning this on.

What are Loyalty Rewards?

Once your completed-order count hits certain milestones (e.g. your 3rd or 5th purchase), a one-time discount code is automatically added to your account — no action needed on your part. You'll find your codes in the Referrals section of your dashboard.

How does the referral reward work?

When a friend signs up with your referral link and buys their first challenge, 10% of that purchase amount is credited directly as cash to your wallet — no code or extra step needed. This reward only applies once, on each referred friend's first purchase.

What are your commissions and spreads?

Our round-turn commission on 1 standard forex lot is just $2.50, with tight spreads (around 0.1 pip on majors like EUR/USD) — among the lowest trading costs in the prop trading industry.
