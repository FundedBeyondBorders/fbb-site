Scaling plan (funded-stage account growth)

Every profit milestone a FUNDED account withdraws grows both its size and the trader's profit split — the same retention mechanic FTMO, The5ers, and FundedNext all use. Triggers automatically at payout time.

Example with defaults: a $10,000 funded account that withdraws $1,000 profit (10%) grows to $12,500 and the trader's split rises from 80% to 85%.

Account reset (breached/expired accounts)

Lets a trader pay a reduced fee to reset a breached or expired account back to its starting state, instead of buying a whole new challenge.
