Tutorial

How do I use the platform?

1

Create an account

Either tap 'Free Test Account', or enter the ID from a challenge you bought.

2

Pick a symbol and direction

Choose a market. If you think the price will go up, tap 'Buy'; otherwise 'Sell'.

3

Set quantity and open

Set the 'Quantity' and tap 'Open Position'.

4

Close the position

Tap 'Close' next to that position in the table.

5

Watch your account status

If a risk rule is violated, the status automatically becomes breached.
