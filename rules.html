Rules

Challenge & Funded Account Rules

Read this page fully before buying any challenge. Payment means you accept these rules.

This page is translated into all 7 site languages.
Account Caps

1Maximum account size

No single challenge account can exceed $500,000.

2Maximum total capital per trader

The combined size of a trader's active/funded accounts cannot exceed $750,000.

3Maximum active accounts

Each trader may hold at most 3 active accounts (challenge or funded) at the same time.

Risk Rules

4Floating Risk Rule

The risk of a single trade or combined simultaneously open trades must not exceed 3% of the account (for sizes $1,000–$50,000) or 2% (for sizes $100,000 and up). Exceeding this limit due to slippage is not a violation; only a deliberate violation (no stop-loss set, combined with real account equity actually dropping past the limit) counts as a violation.

5Minimum trading days

You need at least 3 trading days, each with at least 0.5% profit — to pass any evaluation phase, and also (on the funded account itself) as a condition for your first payout request, on every plan including Instant Funding.

680% Single-Trade Rule

No single trade's profit may represent more than 80% of the phase's total profit (or, on the instant funding plan, total profit since the account started).

740% Consistency Rule (Funded stage)

On a funded account, no single day's profit may exceed 40% of the account's total profit.

General Rules

8No CID sharing

Sharing your account credentials or login details with anyone else is prohibited and results in immediate account closure.

9No hedging

Opening opposite-direction positions on the same symbol or correlated symbols across a trader's own accounts is prohibited. (Group hedging is banned and, if discovered, results in the closure of ALL related accounts.)

10No cross-account copy-trading

Copying trades across multiple accounts to get around the risk rules is prohibited; related accounts are reviewed and may be closed.

11No latency arbitrage

Exploiting delays between price feeds to profit without genuine market risk is prohibited.

12Bots and Expert Advisors

Bots that exploit server bugs are prohibited; ordinary EA use is allowed.

Profit Share & Payouts

13Funded-account profit split

On a funded account, the trader's base share of profit is 75% (one-step plan), 80% (two/three-step plans), or 50% (instant funding plan); the remainder is the platform's share. Each time the account scales up (every 10% of realized profit), this share increases by 5 percentage points, up to a ceiling of 100%.

14Payout schedule

For your first payout, at least 14 days must have passed since your first trade on the funded account. After that, each subsequent payout must be at least 7 days after the previous one. Payment is made within 48 hours of approval.

15Fair & Uniform Execution

Spread, commission, and slippage are applied identically to every trader according to these same fixed, published rules. They are never manually adjusted or targeted to cause a specific trader to fail.

16Definition of "trading day"

The trading day (for the daily loss rule and anywhere else "day" is used) is defined by UTC midnight — not your local time zone.

17Margin Call

If floating losses exceed your free margin, all positions are closed immediately.

18Challenge Fee Refund

Your challenge fee is fully refunded, added on top of your profit share, the first time you successfully request a payout from your funded account.

Violating any of these rules results in automatic account closure and/or manual review by our team, depending on the case.
